Grievance and disciplinary procedure, summary.

Stage one is an informal discussion with the line manager, to be held within
5 working days of the matter being raised. Stage two is a formal hearing
with a manager not previously involved, within 10 working days, with the
right to be accompanied.

Stage three is an appeal to a director, within 10 working days of the stage
two outcome. The director's decision concludes the internal process.

Records of stage two and stage three are retained for 12 months and then
destroyed, except where a sanction remains live.
