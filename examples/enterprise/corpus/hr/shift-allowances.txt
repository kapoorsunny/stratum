Shift allowance schedule, effective January 2026.

Continental shift pattern attracts a 21 percent allowance on base salary.
Night-only pattern attracts 27 percent. Standby, where an engineer is
required to be reachable and within 60 minutes of site, is paid at 145 per
day of standby regardless of whether a call is received.

Callout beyond standby is paid at 1.5 times hourly rate with a minimum of
four hours. The maximum consecutive standby period is 7 days.

Allowances are reviewed each January and are not subject to individual
negotiation.
