Engineering grade bands, 2026.

Grade E1, graduate engineer, band 38,000 to 46,000.
Grade E2, engineer, band 46,000 to 58,000.
Grade E3, senior engineer, band 58,000 to 74,000.
Grade E4, principal engineer, band 74,000 to 95,000.
Grade E5, engineering manager, band 92,000 to 121,000.

Progression between bands requires a completed competency assessment and a
role vacancy. Movement within a band is at manager discretion within the
annual review budget, which for 2026 is 3.1 percent of paybill.

This schedule changes annually and is withdrawn when superseded.
