Site glossary, published.

HRSG, heat recovery steam generator. Recovers heat from gas turbine exhaust
to raise steam.
P and ID, piping and instrumentation diagram. The drawing that shows every
line, valve and instrument in a process.
LOTO, lockout tagout. The procedure for making equipment safe to work on.
MOC, management of change. The process by which a modification is reviewed
before it is made.
TA, turnaround. A planned shutdown for inspection and maintenance.
RCM, reliability centred maintenance. A method for deciding what maintenance
is worth doing based on failure consequence.
