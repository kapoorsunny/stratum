Equipment tag convention, published.

Tags take the form of a letter group, a hyphen, and a number. The letter
group names the equipment class and the number names the unit within an
area.

P   pump
K   compressor
E   heat exchanger
V   vessel
T   tower or column
TX  transformer
CB  circuit breaker

The first digit of the number is the plant area. So P-4471 is a pump in area
4. Tags are unique across the site and are never reused after an item is
removed.
