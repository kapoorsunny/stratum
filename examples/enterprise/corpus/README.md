The synthetic finance and hr documents are here, plus the two written public
documents. The rest of the corpus is public energy engineering material and
is fetched rather than committed, because it is large and it is not ours.

sources.txt in the parent folder lists every URL. Fetch them with
`stratum corpus fetch --urls-file ../sources.txt --out engineering/`.

Every file in finance/ and hr/ is invented. They say so on their first line.
They exist to be things that must not leak.
